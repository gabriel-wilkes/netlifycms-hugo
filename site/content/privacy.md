**Effective date:** November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Azul Technology ("Azul Technology," "we," "us"), operated by Gabriel Wilkes, handles information in connection with:

- the website at [https://azul.technology](https://azul.technology/) (the "Site"), and
- personal Google API applications and OAuth clients that we operate (the "Applications"), including backup and automation tools that access Google user data such as Google Drive files.

The Site is a personal and small-business portfolio. The Applications are personal tools. They access the Google account that authorizes them — typically the account of the owner of Azul Technology — in order to back up or automate that person's own data. They are not used to collect Google user data from the public for advertising or for sale.

## Information we collect

### Information you submit on the Site

If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit so we can read and reply to your inquiry. The form is processed by Netlify Forms and protected by Google reCAPTCHA.

Visitors do not create accounts on the Site. Netlify Identity is used only by the site owner to manage content.

### Information collected automatically on the Site

When you visit the Site, our hosting provider (Netlify) may record standard server and security logs. Those logs can include your IP address, browser type, device type, referring address, the pages requested, and the date and time of the request.

The Site uses Google Analytics to understand how the Site is used, such as pages visited, general geographic region, and browser or device information. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies.

The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to distinguish people from automated spam. reCAPTCHA may process technical information about your browser and device, as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Google user data accessed by the Applications

If you authorize an Application, it can access the Google user data covered by the permissions you approve on Google's consent screen. Depending on the Application, that may include:

- Google Drive files and related metadata (such as file names, types, and modification times) needed to back up, copy, or organize those files
- basic Google account information, such as the email address of the authorized account, needed to identify which account granted access

The Applications request only the access required for the backup or automation feature in use. They do not access Google user data beyond the scopes shown on the consent screen.

## How we use information

We use information from the Site to:

- respond to messages sent through the contact form
- operate, secure, and maintain the Site
- understand aggregate use of the Site through Google Analytics
- reduce spam through Google reCAPTCHA

We use Google user data received through the Applications only to provide or improve the user-facing feature the authorizing user requested. For these tools, that means personal backup and automation of that user's own Google data, such as copying or organizing that user's own Google Drive files.

We do not use personal information or Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

### Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

Azul Technology's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular:

- We use Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features of the Application the user authorized.
- We do not sell Google user data.
- We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized advertising, or interest-based advertising.
- We do not use or transfer Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
- We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless you have given affirmative consent to view specific data, it is necessary for security (such as investigating abuse), it is necessary to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and used for internal operations in accordance with applicable law.
- We do not transfer Google user data to others except as allowed by the Limited Use requirements: to provide or improve the user-facing features you authorized, for security, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with your prior consent where that policy requires consent.

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information or Google user data with third parties for their own marketing, advertising, or independent commercial use.

We disclose information only in these cases:

- **Service providers for the Site.** Netlify hosts the Site and processes contact-form submissions and hosting logs on our behalf. Google provides Analytics and reCAPTCHA. These providers process Site data so we can operate the Site. Google user data accessed by the Applications is not sent to Netlify and is not sold or shared with a third party for that party's own use.
- **The authorizing user.** Google user data accessed by an Application is handled for the Google account that granted access. It is not disclosed to other people.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Azul Technology, our users, or the public.
- **Business transfer.** If Azul Technology is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction. Google user data is transferred only with any prior consent the Google API Services User Data Policy requires.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

- **Contact messages.** We keep contact-form submissions only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation, and then we delete them.
- **Hosting logs.** Netlify retains server logs according to its ordinary operational schedule.
- **Analytics.** Google Analytics data is retained according to the retention setting on our Analytics property.
- **Google user data.** We retain Google user data only for as long as needed to perform the backup or automation that was requested. Copies created by a personal backup tool remain under the control of the account that authorized the Application, and that person can delete them. We do not build advertising profiles from Google user data, and we do not keep it in order to sell it.

You can revoke an Application's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page: [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access stops further collection.

To ask us to delete personal information or Google user data we still hold, email [gabriel@azultech.com](mailto:gabriel@azultech.com). We will delete it unless we must keep a specific record to comply with law, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements. Files you stored on your own devices or accounts are deleted by you.

- [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) — hosting, form handling, and, for the site owner only, identity and content management
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) — Google Analytics, Google reCAPTCHA, and Google APIs (including Google Drive) when an Application is authorized
- [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) — including reCAPTCHA
- [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy) — including the Limited Use requirements, for Google user data

Links from the Site to other websites, such as portfolio projects or social profiles, are governed by those sites' own policies. This policy does not cover them.

## Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a personal and small-business site, including HTTPS and access limited to the account owner for the Applications. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Children

The Site and the Applications are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy at [https://azul.technology/privacy/](https://azul.technology/privacy/).
